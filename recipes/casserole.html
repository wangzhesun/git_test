Casserole Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 can of cream of mushroom soup
  • 2 cups of cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup of frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 cup of shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup of crushed crackers

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, mix the cream of mushroom soup, chicken, mixed vegetables, and half of the cheese.
  3. Pour the mixture into a greased casserole dish.
  4. Top with crushed crackers and remaining cheese.
  5. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until bubbly and golden brown.
